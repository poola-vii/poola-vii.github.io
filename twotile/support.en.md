As of TwoTile 1.0.9 (public beta).

## How to contact us

There are two contacts, shared by all software made by poola-vii. **We recommend the first one, <https://github.com/poola-vii/support>.** Whichever you use, **make it clear that your message is about TwoTile**.

| Contact | Best for |
|:---|:---|
| **1. <https://github.com/poola-vii/support> (recommended)** | It has a form for each kind of request, so you can include everything we need. You can also read other people's reports and our replies |
| 2. Email (`poola.vii.support@gmail.com`) | For people who don't have, or don't want, a GitHub account |

| Request | How to send it |
|:---|:---|
| Bug reports, questions about how to use TwoTile, and feature requests | Post from **Issues** in the first repository, choosing one of the forms. **Posting requires a GitHub account** (free). If you don't have one, email is fine too |
| Security vulnerability reports | Send them with **Private Vulnerability Reporting** from the **Security** tab of the first repository. **Don't post them in a public issue.** If you don't have a GitHub account, send them by email |
| Something you wrote in a Microsoft Store review | When we notice it, we reply publicly and point you to these contacts, because Store reviews don't allow one-to-one conversations |

We don't make public what you send us by email. If we find that it affects other users too, we may write about it in the first repository or in [Known Issues](known_issues.en.md), in a way that doesn't identify you.

### Please don't include

Anyone can read public issues. Don't include the following. Even by email, don't send credentials or raw logs.

- **Raw logs**: As described in [How to Collect Logs](log_collection.en.md), read them and remove anything private before you paste them
- **Personal information**: your name, address, phone number, email address, workplace, and so on
- **Credentials**: passwords, tokens, API keys, and so on
- **Details of vulnerabilities that haven't been made public**: use Private Vulnerability Reporting

If we find that a post includes any of these, we may edit or delete it.

### What to tell us

See §3 of [How to Collect Logs](log_collection.en.md). With these three things, **what you did and what happened, the TwoTile version, and the Windows version**, we can look into most problems.

## Scope of support

### What we do

- Look into bugs in the latest version released as a public beta
- Answer questions about how to use TwoTile
- Keep [Known Issues](known_issues.en.md) up to date
- Fix what we decide to fix (in the next version)

### What we don't do

These are things **we can't do**. It doesn't mean we take your report lightly.

- **Support for old versions.** Please check whether the problem also happens in the latest version before you tell us.
- **Environments we've decided not to support.** ARM PCs, 32-bit Windows, and operating systems other than Windows.
- **Anything listed under "By design" in [Known Issues](known_issues.en.md).** We chose that behavior for a reason. We're still happy to hear your opinion.
- **Custom work for a particular environment.** We don't add features just for one specific app.
- **Sorting out problems caused by combining TwoTile with other software.** If you run TwoTile with another window manager, they fight over the windows.
- **Support by phone or in person.**

### What we can't promise

**This public beta is made by an individual in their spare time and distributed free of charge.**

- **We can't promise how quickly we'll reply.** It may take days or weeks, whichever contact you use.
- **We can't promise to act on every report.** We may decide not to fix something. If so, we'll tell you why.
- **We can't promise to keep distributing TwoTile.** We may stop. If we do, we'll announce it at the download source.
- **During the public beta, behavior may change without notice.** Big changes are noted in the [Changelog](CHANGELOG.en.md).

For details, see the [Terms of Use](terms.en.md).

## Language

**We accept questions in Japanese and English.** Replies to questions in English may take longer than replies in Japanese.
