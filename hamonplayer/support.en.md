This applies to HamonPlayer 1.0.2 (public beta).

## How to contact us

There are two support contacts, shared by all software made by poola-vii. **We recommend the first one, <https://github.com/poola-vii/support>.** Whichever you use, **make it clear that you're writing about HamonPlayer.**

| Contact | Best for |
|:---|:---|
| **1. <https://github.com/poola-vii/support> (recommended)** | It has a form for each kind of request, so you can include everything we need. You can also read other people's reports and our replies |
| 2. Email (`poola.vii.support@gmail.com`) | For people who don't have, or don't want, a GitHub account |

| What | How to send it |
|:---|:---|
| Bug reports, questions, and requests | Post from **Issues** in the first repository, using one of the forms. **You need a GitHub account to post** (it's free). If you don't have one, email is fine too |
| Security vulnerabilities | Send them through **Private Vulnerability Reporting** on the **Security** tab of the first repository. **Don't post them in a public issue.** If you don't have a GitHub account, send them by email |
| Feedback you wrote in a Microsoft Store review | When we notice it, we'll reply publicly and point you to this contact, because Store reviews don't allow one-to-one conversations |

We don't make public what you send us by email. If we find that it affects other users too, we may write about it in the first repository or in [Known issues](known_issues.en.md), in a way that doesn't identify you.

**You can contact us in English or Japanese.** Replies to questions in English may take longer than replies in Japanese.

### Don't include

Anyone can read public issues. Don't include the following. Even by email, don't send credentials.

- **Personal information**, such as names, addresses, phone numbers, email addresses, or employers
- **Logs you haven't read and checked.** The log includes the locations of your music folders, which may include your Windows user name. Follow [How to collect logs](log_collection.en.md): read the log, and remove anything you don't want to share before you paste it
- **Song files.** Don't attach song files. If you attach a screenshot, hide any song names or folder names you don't want to share
- **Credentials**, such as passwords, tokens, or API keys
- **Undisclosed security vulnerabilities**. Use Private Vulnerability Reporting instead

If we find a post that includes any of these, we may edit or delete it.

### Please include

With these three things, we can usually look into the problem:

- **What you did and what happened.** Step by step, if you can, and when it happened.
- **The HamonPlayer version.** Right-click the icon and open **Help**. The version is on the last line (for example, `HamonPlayer 1.0.2`). Also tell us whether you use the Microsoft Store version or the setup.exe version.
- **The Windows version.** Edition, Version, and OS build, under Settings > System > About.

If the icon is red, it also helps to know the message you see when you point at the icon. If we need the log to look into the problem, see [How to collect logs](log_collection.en.md).

## What we support

### What we do

- Investigate bugs in the latest version released as the public beta
- Answer questions about how to use it
- Keep the known issues up to date
- Fix the problems we decide to fix (in the next version)

### What we don't do

These are things **we can't do**. They don't mean we take your report lightly.

- **Support older versions.** Check whether the problem also happens in the latest version first.
- **Support for operating systems other than Windows.** We'll update this when the phone version (Android) is released.
- **Change behavior listed under "By design" in the known issues.** We chose it for a reason. We're still happy to hear your feedback.
- **Help with getting, converting, or tagging song files.** HamonPlayer only plays the song files you have.
- **Integrate with music streaming services.** HamonPlayer only plays the song files in your music folders.
- **Phone or in-person support.**

### What we can't promise

**The public beta is made by an individual in their spare time and distributed free of charge.**

- **We can't promise how quickly we'll reply.** It may take days or weeks, whichever contact you use.
- **We can't promise to act on every report.** We may decide not to fix something. If so, we'll tell you why.
- **We can't promise to keep distributing it.** We may stop. If we do, we'll announce it on the distribution site.
- **Behavior may change without notice during the public beta.** We'll list major changes in the [changelog](CHANGELOG.en.md).

For details, see the [Terms of Use](terms.en.md).
